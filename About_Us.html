WHO WE ARE

Welcome to Sunrise Community Hospital

Sunrise Community Hospital is committed to providing compassionate, patient-centred healthcare in a welcoming and supportive environment.

We believe that quality healthcare goes beyond treating illness. It also means listening to patients, respecting their needs, providing clear information, and supporting families throughout their healthcare journey.

Our approach puts people first. We strive to make every patient feel heard, respected, and cared for, while promoting health, well-being, and a stronger community.
