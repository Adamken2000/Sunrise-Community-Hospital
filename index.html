Welcome to Sunrise Community Hospital

Caring for Life, Every Step of the Way

Founded with a commitment to health and community well-being, Sunrise Community Hospital has been providing accessible, compassionate, and high-quality medical care to families across the region for over a decade. What began as a modest outpatient clinic has grown into a modern, fully equipped healthcare facility designed to support every stage of your healing journey.
